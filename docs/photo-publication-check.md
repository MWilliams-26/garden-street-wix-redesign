## Publication practice

- Prefer approved web-ready working copies over HEIC, AVIF or oversized camera originals.
- Use important program facts as webpage text, never only as text inside a flyer or photo.
- Treat images with turned-away or partially obscured children as requiring approval; lack of a clear face is not automatic publication clearance.
- Record final selected asset path, destination, alt text, approval and placement in the applicable inventory before launch.
