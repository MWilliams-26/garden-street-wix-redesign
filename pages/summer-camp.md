## Responsive recommendations

### Desktop

Use a simple two-column overview with confirmed information beside a restrained gallery; keep dates and actions visible as real text.

### Tablet

Verify the desktop Wix Editor outcome in portrait and landscape; prevent cards and galleries from becoming cramped.

### Mobile

Stack the overview before the gallery, then show the confirmed date and mailing-list action. Keep timing and next action scannable.
