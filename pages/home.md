## Responsive recommendations

### Desktop

Use clear hierarchy, concise pathways, and visible primary action without overcrowding the hero.

### Tablet

Preserve reading order and avoid narrow multi-column cards.

### Mobile

Lead with the key parent benefit and a large, immediate action; stack sections in priority order.
